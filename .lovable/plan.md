
# Dexma | Minecraft Developer Portfolio

A stunning, animated dark-themed portfolio with purple/blue accents, inspired by the reference site but elevated with modern design and rich animations.

---

## 1. Hero Section (Full-Screen Landing)
- Full-viewport dark hero with a Minecraft-themed background (gradient/particle effects instead of a static image)
- Floating particle animation throughout the section
- Bold title: **"Dexma | Minecraft Server Developer"** with a glowing purple/blue text effect
- Tagline with typewriter animation
- Smooth scroll-down CTA button with glow effect

## 2. About Me Section
- Your uploaded profile image displayed in a stylish circular frame with a glowing purple border/ring animation
- Short bio text about your Minecraft development experience
- Fade-in and slide animations on scroll

## 3. Projects Section
- Grid of project cards with glassmorphism styling (frosted glass effect)
- Each card shows: project name, status badge (Active/Offline/Coming Soon), and your role
- **BasakMC** featured prominently with "Head Developer" role
- Cards animate in on scroll with staggered fade-in effects
- Hover effects: cards lift up with a purple glow shadow

## 4. Skills & Experience Section
- Animated skill bars or progress indicators with purple/blue gradient fills
- Skills like Server Setups, Plugin Configurations, Server Management, etc.
- Each bar animates when scrolled into view

## 5. Contact Section
- Clean contact cards for Email and Discord
- Hover glow effects on contact cards
- Smooth links to email and Discord profile

## 6. Global Design & Animations
- **Color palette**: Deep dark background (#0a0a1a) with purple (#8b5cf6) and blue (#3b82f6) accents
- Smooth scroll behavior across all sections
- Parallax-like scroll effects
- Floating particles/stars background animation throughout the page
- Sticky navigation bar with blur backdrop that highlights the current section
- Fully responsive (mobile-friendly)
